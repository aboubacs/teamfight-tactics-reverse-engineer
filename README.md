##About

This project is a complete reverse engineering of Teamfight Tactics as it was in 2021 (Sets 4.5 and 5).

The goal is to simulate fights and gather data in under a second, rather than having to play full 30–40-minute games in the live version.

Champions, movement mechanics, mana generation, shields, damage calculations, projectiles, positioning, items, and synergies are all fully implemented.

This allows data to be collected at a speed unmatched by the live game, as illustrated in the image below: a single fight (left) is simulated hundreds of times to determine which side comes out ahead (right).
